2016 request:

Please provide the original KML/vector dataset, metadata and observation dates/times for each feature. Please confirm its lineage and whether it is the same dataset as the Survey/Navy map. We cannot transfer metadata between these products without that confirmation. Please also advise on source feature OBJECTID 48912, which has a self-intersecting ring; we have not repaired the source geometry.

2017 request:
